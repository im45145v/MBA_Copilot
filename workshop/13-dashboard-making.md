# Hands-on Exercise

## Step 1 — Generate the Executive Report

Use your **Executive Report Prompt File**.

Ask Copilot:

```text
Use the Executive Report prompt to generate a board-ready report using the latest dashboard insights.
```

Your report should include:

- Executive Summary
- Key KPIs
- Business Performance
- Financial Highlights
- Risks
- Opportunities
- Strategic Recommendations

Review the report for accuracy before accepting it.

---

## Step 2 — Ask Every Specialist

Now consult your AI team.

Executive Advisor

```text
What are the three highest-priority strategic recommendations for Nova Retail?
```

Financial Analyst

```text
Summarise the company's financial performance and identify the largest profitability opportunities.
```

Marketing Strategist

```text
Recommend customer growth initiatives based on the dashboard insights.
```

Operations Consultant

```text
Identify operational improvements that could increase efficiency.
```

Notice how each Agent contributes a different perspective.

---

## Step 3 — Create an Executive Summary

Ask Copilot:

```text
Summarise the entire project into one page.

The audience is the Board of Directors.

Focus on:

- business impact
- major findings
- recommendations

Avoid technical implementation details.
```

The best executive summaries are concise, clear and action-oriented.

---

## Step 4 — Generate Presentation Content

Ask:

```text
Create a 10-slide executive presentation.

Include:

- agenda
- business overview
- KPI highlights
- trends
- risks
- opportunities
- recommendations
- next steps

Each slide should contain concise speaker notes.
```

You now have presentation content ready for tools like PowerPoint or Google Slides.

---

## Step 5 — Create an Action Plan

Finally ask your Executive Advisor:

```text
Convert today's recommendations into a 90-day business action plan.

Group actions into:

- Immediate
- Short Term
- Long Term

Prioritise by business impact.
```

This transforms analysis into execution.
