# The Biggest Lesson

Most people think AI replaces work.

The best professionals use AI to **amplify** their work.

Throughout this workshop, you remained responsible for:

- understanding the business problem
- asking the right questions
- reviewing plans
- approving changes
- validating outputs
- making final decisions

GitHub Copilot accelerated your work.

It didn't replace your judgement.
