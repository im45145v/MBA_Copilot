You joined **Nova Retail Ltd.** last week as a **Business Analyst**.

> **Subject:** Urgent: Board Meeting Tomorrow
>
> Good morning,
>
> Tomorrow's board meeting has been moved forward.

# Your Role

For the rest of this workshop, **you are the Business Analyst** responsible for preparing tomorrow's board presentation.

You'll use GitHub Copilot to accelerate your work—not to replace your judgement.

# About Nova Retail

Nova Retail is a multinational retail company that sells products across multiple markets.

The company has experienced rapid growth, but leadership has noticed several warning signs.

Current concerns include:

* Revenue is increasing
* Profit margins are shrinking
* Shipping costs appear unusually high
* Some regions consistently underperform
* Discounting has increased significantly
* Management lacks clear visibility into business performance

Your task is to investigate these issues.

# Success Criteria

A successful solution should answer questions like:

* Which regions are losing money?
* Which products are the most profitable?
* Where should the company invest next?
* Are discounts reducing profitability?
* Which customers generate the highest value?
* What should the CEO prioritise next quarter?

If your dashboard helps answer these questions, you've succeeded.

# Think Like an Analyst

Before asking AI anything, spend one minute thinking about the problem.

Ask yourself:

* What would a CEO care about?
* What would a CFO want to know?
* Which KPIs would help management make decisions?
* What information would convince the board?

Remember:

> **AI can generate insights, but it's your responsibility to ask the right questions.**
